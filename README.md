# Smokeleaf Plus

A premium, low-impairment alternative to vanilla smokeleaf. The Advanced Smokeleaf Joint delivers the same mood boost as a regular joint, but with almost no downside — perfect for colonists who need to stay functional.

## Features

- **Same +13 mood** as a regular smokeleaf joint
- **~83% less consciousness penalty** (−5% vs −30%) and **no movement penalty**
- Same **−20% pain relief** as regular smokeleaf
- **Gentle wakeful edge**: slightly reduced sleep fall rate (95%)
- **Mild munchies**: +10% hunger rate
- **Lower addiction and tolerance risk** than regular joints
- Crafted at the **Drug Lab** after researching **Smokeleaf Refinement**

## Effects

| | Smokeleaf joint | Advanced Smokeleaf Joint |
|---|---|---|
| Mood | +13 | +13 |
| Consciousness | −30% | −5% |
| Moving | −10% | none |
| Hunger | +30% | +10% |
| Pain | −20% | −20% |
| Sleep fall rate | 100% | 95% |
| Tolerance per dose | 3% | 1% |

## Recipe

- 8x smokeleaf leaves
- Drug Lab, Cooking skill

## Research

**Smokeleaf Refinement** (Industrial tech, 1000 research points)

- Requires: Drug Production
- Positioned next to Psychite Refining in the research tree

## Addiction profile

Shares the vanilla Smokeleaf chemical. Lower risk than regular joints:

- Addictiveness: 0.015 (vs 0.020)
- Minimum tolerance to addict: 20% (vs 15%)
- Tolerance per dose: 1% (vs 3%)

## Installation

1. Download the mod (Steam Workshop, or clone this repo)
2. Copy the folder to `RimWorld/Mods/`
3. Enable **Smokeleaf Plus** in the Mods menu

## Compatibility

- RimWorld 1.5 and 1.6
- XML-only — no C# required
